## Feature rationale register

This document records why each possible variable may be included, where the data may come from, how it will be converted into weekly data, and whether it will be used in the final dataset.

| Variable | Expected link with USD/IRR volatility | Why it may matter for Iran |  Data source | Original frequency | Weekly preparation |  Data delay | Available period | Decision
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Sanctions events | New sanctions may increase uncertainty and exchange-rate volatility, while relief may reduce pressure | Sanctions can affect Iran’s oil income, financial access, trade and foreign-currency availability | Pesaran, US Treasury OFAC | Event-based | Count events by week and create separate tightening and relief indicators | sanctions events are assigned using their official announcement date, so no additional publication lag is required. | OFAC archives cover the full 2011–2026 study period | Include
| Inflation | Higher inflation may increase pressure on the rial and contribute to exchange-rate instability. | Iran has experienced persistent and sometimes high inflation, which may affect confidence in the rial and demand for foreign currency. | IMF Consumer Price Index (CPI) dataset | Monthly | Calculate year-on-year inflation from the monthly all-items CPI index, then carry the latest available value forward to weekly observations. | year-on-year inflation is lagged by one month before being aligned with the weekly dataset to reduce the risk of future-data leakage. | 2010-M12 to 2026-M06 in downloaded CPI series; usable YoY inflation from 2011-M12 | Include
| Brent Crude oil prices | Changes in oil prices may affect Iran's foreign-currency income and economic conditions, which could influence USD/IRR volatility. | Oil exports are important to the Iranian economy, so large changes in global oil prices may affect foreign-exchange conditions. | U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Weekly Europe Brent Spot Price FOB | Weekly | Use the official EIA weekly average price and calculate the weekly Brent log return. No daily-to-weekly resampling is required. | the weekly Brent value is treated as available at the end of its Friday-ending week and no additional lag is applied. | 1987 onwards; restrict processed data to the USD/IRR study period | Include as candidate explanatory variable.
